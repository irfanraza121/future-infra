Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: March 2026. This policy governs booking cancellations and refunds.

1. Booking Cancellation

A booking may be cancelled by the applicant by submitting a written request to Future Homes Infra along with the original receipt and booking documents. Cancellation is effective from the date the written request is received.

2. Refund Eligibility

Refund eligibility depends on the stage of the transaction:

  • Before agreement: booking amount refunded after deduction of administrative charges.
  • After agreement to sale: refund governed by the terms of the executed agreement.
  • After sale deed: no refund is applicable; the transaction is complete.

3. Processing

Approved refunds are processed within 30 to 45 working days from approval, through banking channels to the account of the original payer. Processing time may vary based on banking procedures.

4. Administrative Charges

Applicable administrative, documentation and statutory charges will be deducted from the refundable amount as per the booking terms and the executed agreement.

5. Exceptions

Refunds may be declined or adjusted where the applicant has not complied with payment schedules, where statutory deductions apply, or where the property has already been transferred or possession handed over.

6. Contact

For cancellation requests and refund status, contact sales@futurehomesinfra.in or call +91 98765 43210 during business hours.
