1. Information Collection

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when you submit an enquiry, request a quotation, book a site visit or contact us. This may include:

  • Name, phone number and email address
  • Property preferences such as project, budget and unit type
  • Communication records with our sales executives
  • Technical data such as browser type, device type and pages visited

2. Data Usage

Your information is used to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, share project updates, arrange site visits and improve our services. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, measure traffic and improve the browsing experience. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings; some features may not function correctly if disabled.

4. Data Security

We apply reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect your information against unauthorised access, alteration or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

5. Third Party Services

We may use trusted third-party providers for hosting, analytics, communication and payment verification. These providers process data only for the purpose of delivering services to us and are bound by confidentiality obligations.

6. User Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you may opt out of promotional communication at any time by contacting us using the details below.

7. Mobile & Device Policy

This section describes how the website behaves on mobile phones, tablets and other devices:

  • Responsive experience: layouts adapt to your screen size. Tables may scroll horizontally and forms switch to a single column on small screens.
  • Device permissions: the website does not access your camera, microphone, contacts or storage without your explicit action.
  • Notifications: we do not send push notifications unless you explicitly subscribe to a service that provides them.
  • WhatsApp communication: when you choose to chat via the WhatsApp widget, you are redirected to WhatsApp and its own privacy terms apply.
  • Data light usage: the site is optimised to load efficiently on mobile data connections.
  • Children: this website is not directed at persons under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
